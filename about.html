About Rastranirman Secondary School

Our story, philosophy, and commitment

Founded in 2054 B.S., Rastranirman Secondary School has been a beacon of quality education in the Timtine, Ratuwamai-10 region of Morang. For nearly three decades, we have dedicated ourselves to nurturing young minds and building strong foundations for future leaders.

Our educational philosophy centers on holistic development — combining academic excellence with character building, discipline, and co-curricular activities. We believe every child has unique potential, and our role is to provide the environment and guidance for that potential to flourish.

With classes from Nursery through Grade 12, we offer a complete educational journey. Our commitment to students extends beyond textbooks, fostering critical thinking, creativity, and values that prepare them for higher education and life beyond school.

Our Guiding Principles

The foundation of everything we do

To provide quality education that empowers students with knowledge, skills, and values necessary to succeed in a rapidly changing world.

Vision

To be a center of academic excellence that nurtures innovative thinkers, compassionate leaders, and responsible citizens.

To develop academic proficiency, foster moral values, encourage creative expression, and prepare students for higher education.
